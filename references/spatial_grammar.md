# Spatial grammar

For Planet Rebus / general Russian rebuses test, when geometry supports it:

- И
- К
- ЗА
- В
- НА
- НЕ
- ОТ
- У
- ПО
- С
- ПОД
- НАД
- ПЕРЕД
- ИЗ
- МЕЖДУ
- ЧЕРЕЗ

A pointing finger/hand toward a target can encode `К`.

Do not reduce spatial layouts to simple left-to-right concatenation until relation readings have been tested.
